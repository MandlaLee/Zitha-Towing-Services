From a sedan that will not start on the R71 to a truck that has gone off the shoulder outside town. If it needs to be moved, loaded, or stored, call the dispatch line.

03 / Dispatch process

Phone answered. Unit dispatched. Vehicle secured.

Zitha's Towing Services is a locally based operator, not a national call centre. When you phone 072 081 9170, you speak to someone who knows Phalaborwa’s roads, the R71 traffic, and where the nearest safe offloading point is.

We ask for the vehicle type, location, and what happened. Then we tell you what we are sending and when it will arrive. If the job needs a flatbed, a heavy-duty unit, or long-distance transport, dispatch will confirm the right equipment before the unit leaves the yard.

For accident recoveries, we work with insurance-approved processes and provide secure storage at our Bataleur Street premises. Vehicles are logged and released only with authorised instruction.

  1. Call or WhatsApp

    Phone the dispatch line or send a WhatsApp message. Give the location, vehicle type, and a short description of the problem.

  2. Get a clear quote

    Dispatch confirms the call-out fee, towing rate, and any storage costs before a unit is sent. No surprise charges at the other end.

  3. Unit dispatched

    The nearest available truck is sent. You are told the unit type and an estimated arrival window based on where you are.

  4. Load, secure, deliver

    The vehicle is loaded, tied down, and transported to your chosen workshop, panel beater, or our secure storage facility.
